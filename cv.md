  <div class="body">
    <h2>Research profile</h2>
    <p>My work focuses on the design and optimization of <strong>fusion power plants under physics uncertainty</strong>: risk-informed, data-driven, multi-objective design of complete tokamaks and their subsystems (plasma shape, magnets, power exhaust), and the integration of plasma-physics constraints with thermal, structural, and cost limits through AI-enabled surrogate modeling. All of it is grounded in experimental magnetic confinement fusion research. Read more on the <a href="{{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}">Research</a> page, or download my <a href="{{ site.cv-pdf | relative_url }}">CV (PDF)</a>.</p>
