<div class="section" id="students">
  <div class="label"><div class="num">01</div><div class="t">Early career</div></div>
  <div class="body">
    <h2>Supporting students and early-career scientists</h2>
    <p>Since 2020, I have chaired the APS Division of Plasma Physics <strong>CONNECT</strong> committee, the national body that represents students and early-career plasma scientists. Our volunteer committee gathers community data on early-career experiences, organizes recurring professional development events, and partners with the APS-DPP Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Organizing Collective Committee to make the field more accessible.</p>
    <p>I helped establish, and from 2021 to 2024 chaired, the annual <strong>APS-DPP Student Day</strong>, a mini-conference at the division's national meeting where students present their research to peers, meet laboratory and industry scientists, and talk openly about mental health and belonging in the field. I am also a trained member of the APS-DPP Ally Program.</p>
    <p>As a graduate student at Princeton, I founded the <strong>Plasma Graduate Student Committee</strong>, which gave students a formal voice in curriculum reform and remains active today. I also mentored in Princeton's PreDoc graduate preparation program and served on the board of the international student-led Fusion EP seminar series.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section" id="public">
  <div class="label"><div class="num">02</div><div class="t">Public</div></div>
  <div class="body">
    <h2>Broadening engagement with fusion</h2>
    <p>I co-chair and organize <a href="https://www.fusionenergyweek.org/"><strong>Fusion Energy Week</strong></a>, a global outreach series held each May, and co-lead <a href="https://usfusionenergy.org/">USFusionEnergy.org</a> and the US Fusion Outreach Team, which coordinate grassroots fusion education nationwide. In 2026 I became <strong>Outreach Chair of the MagNetUS Executive Committee</strong>, which develops plasma education and training programs across the US.</p>
    <p>With an APS Forum on Outreach and Engaging the Public minigrant, I designed and distributed <a href="{{ '/2024-03-15-tradingcards/' | relative_url }}"><strong>Fusion Trading Cards</strong></a> that introduce students to fusion science concepts. I regularly give public talks, including at the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zuvT9qhCfR8">CU Boulder Saturday Physics Series</a>, at <a href="{{ '/2026-07-21-colorado-fusion-week/' | relative_url }}">Colorado Fusion Week</a>, and for Colorado high school students. I am a member of the Small College Plasma Consortium, which shares teaching resources with institutions that lack large plasma programs.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="section" id="research-community">
  <div class="label"><div class="num">03</div><div class="t">Research community</div></div>
  <div class="body">
    <h2>Leadership in the research community</h2>
    <p>I co-chair the <strong>General Atomics Personnel Development Committee</strong>, which works to improve community, training, and professional climate at the DIII-D National Fusion Facility, the largest magnetic fusion user facility in the US. I lead the joint EU/US working group on negative triangularity, and previously led a working group of the US Joint Research Taskforce on ELM-free regimes.</p>
    <p>I also enjoy building student-led research efforts. In 2022, I was project lead for a team of eight graduate students in the ORFEAS Fusion Design Contest, which produced a roadmap for bringing fusion to the US grid under economic, manufacturing, and engineering constraints and won the top prize. I have since helped organize two inter-institutional fusion design courses, both of which led to peer-reviewed papers written by students.</p>
  </div>
</div>
